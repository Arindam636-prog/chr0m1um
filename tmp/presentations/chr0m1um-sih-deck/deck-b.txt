CONTEXT SHIELD
(Privacy-Preserving Context Firewall)
- Innovation
▸ Excessive Context Exposure : AI agents may receive more webpage data than required.
▸ Sensitive Data Risk : Private information can unintentionally enter the AI context.
▸ Privacy vs Capability : Removing too much context can reduce task effectiveness.
▸ Privacy-Aware Context Firewall : Controls what browser information reaches the AI agent.
▸ Local Context Protection : Detects and sanitizes unnecessary sensitive information before AI access.
▸ Minimum Necessary Context : Preserves task-relevant information while reducing exposure.
▸ Task-Aware Privacy : Instead of blindly masking data, ContextShield considers the task before deciding what the AI
should see.
▸ Privacy Before AI Access : Protection happens before context reaches the AI planner.
▸ Controlled AI Actions : AI-generated browser actions pass through a validation layer before execution.

I M P A C T S & B E N E F I T S
● The Privacy & Trust Impact : AI agents can become more trustworthy when users do not have to
expose their complete browser context for every task. ContextShield promotes the principle of minimum
necessary context before information reaches an AI system.
● The Security & Responsible Automation Impact : Sensitive information is analyzed before AI
access, while generated actions can be validated before execution. This adds protection at both sides
of the agent workflow — what the AI sees and what the AI is allowed to do.
● The Adoption & Ecosystem Impact : A reusable context-control layer can support individuals and
organizations in adopting browser-based AI assistance with greater confidence, and can provide a
foundation for privacy-aware AI-agent systems.
