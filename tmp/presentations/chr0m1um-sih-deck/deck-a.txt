PROPOSED SOLUTION
Cross-Platform One-Click Wipe : Works on Windows, Linux & Android, even in hidden areas (HPA/DCO, SSD sectors).
Tamper-Proof Digital Certificate : Generates signed wipe proof (PDF/JSON) with QR for instant online verification.
Device Passport : Auto-captures CPU, RAM & serial info for authenticity and resale trust.
Three-Mode Breakthrough : Online, Offline & Direct E-Waste modes cover every scenario - working + dead devices.
UVP (UNIQUE VALUE PROPOSITION)
World’s First App-Generated USB Bootable : Make your own wipe tool at home. USB drive. No cost. No experts required.
Device Passport + Proof of Wipe : Every device gets a digital passport with CPU/RAM/serial details + a tamper-proof
certificate for resale trust.
Cross-Platform & Offline : Runs anywhere unlike expensive complex tools.
Simple, Secure & Rewarding : Easy-to-use solution that safely wipes data while giving users Eco points for disposal.

The Economic & Job Creation Impact : Hundreds of millions of old laptops and phones are sitting idle.
By securely selling/recycling these devices, we can recover ₹50,000 crore in value and generate over
25,000+ direct jobs in e-waste sector within 3 years.

Environmental & Sustainability Gains : 5 lakh tonnes of e-waste through nationwide goes to landfills
every year. Proper recycling of devices gives an electronic device a second life, reduces pollution to our

Social & Governance Transformation : People worry about data theft when recycling devices. Verified
wipe certificates and rewards make it safe and encouraging. The project also opens jobs for women and
rural areas and supports Digital India & Swachh Bharat.
